        Ground rules

        Copy the structure. Never the film.

        Every breakdown here describes how a piece was built — the order of the beats, where the tension sits, what the camera does. That's craft, and craft is meant to be studied and reused. The scripts, footage, music, characters, and taglines are not: they're owned, and lifting them gets your ad pulled and your client sued.

        A workable test before you shoot: if someone who has seen the original watches yours and thinks “same trick, different product”, you're fine. If they think “that's the Volvo ad”, you're not.

        About the view counts

        Every figure here is read live from the YouTube Data API against the brand's own upload, so it is exact rather than estimated — and it will have moved by the time you read it.

        The library is deliberately short. Plenty of famous campaigns are missing because the brands deleted their own uploads, and the only surviving copies are third-party re-uploads whose counts are off by up to a thousandfold. Rather than publish a number that wrong, those entries are held back until the original reappears.

        One caveat that applies to all of them: a single upload's count is not a campaign's reach. Broadcast, paid social and re-cuts are not in these numbers.
