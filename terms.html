1. What this service is

Adventure Advertising automatically searches public sources — the YouTube Data API and the Meta Ad Library — for the most-viewed and fastest-spreading advertising, and publishes an analysis of how each campaign is constructed: its beats, its timings, and the mechanism that made it work.

It is a research and education tool. It is not an advertising agency, not a media-buying service, and not professional advice on what your business should spend.

5. Accuracy, and its limits

We are deliberate about this, because the whole point of the site is that the numbers are real:

  • View counts come live from the YouTube Data API and are exact at the moment they were fetched. The page shows when that was.
  • Meta figures are EU reach only. Meta publishes no other reach number anywhere in the world, including India. We label this rather than implying global figures.
  • High view counts can be bought. YouTube counts paid placements in the public total, so a big number may measure media budget rather than genuine interest. Where the engagement rate suggests this, we flag it.
  • We verify before we publish. If the only surviving copy of an ad is a third-party re-upload, we do not present its view count as the campaign's reach.
  • Third-party platforms can change, restrict or withdraw their data at any time, and their availability is outside our control.

Analyses represent our reading of publicly visible work. They are commentary and opinion, not statements of fact about any brand's internal strategy, budget or results.
